Lumen is a calm, private workspace for everything you plan, write and automate.

PLAN
• Capture tasks in plain language: "Call Sam tomorrow at 5pm !high #family" understands the date, time, priority and tag.
• Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Inbox and Anytime lists, projects with progress, subtasks, dependencies and repeating tasks.
• A calendar with week and month views that works with your system calendars.
• Reminders that respect quiet hours.

WRITE
• Markdown notes with wiki links, backlinks, tags and version history.
• Import documents, preview them, and find the text inside PDFs.
• Collections to keep everything in its place.

FIND
• Universal search across tasks, notes, documents and commands, with a command palette.

AUTOMATE
• Build automations visually: triggers, conditions, delays and actions, with a detailed history of every run.

ASK
• An assistant that can plan your day, find a note or create tasks for you — with your own Anthropic or OpenAI key, and your approval before it changes anything.

UNDERSTAND
• Insights about your work: streaks, completion trends and weekly reports, computed on your device.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays on your device. No account, no tracking, no analytics leave the device.
• API keys are stored only in the Keychain.

Built for iPhone, with widgets, Shortcuts, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support.
